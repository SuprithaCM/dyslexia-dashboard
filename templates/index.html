Eye-Tracking Dyslexia Screening

You will calibrate the webcam (≈1 min), then silently read 3 short passages. Gaze is analysed locally on this server; no video is stored. Research screening only — not a clinical diagnosis.

Tips: face a light source, keep your head still and ~60 cm from the screen, use a laptop/desktop with a webcam, close other tabs.
